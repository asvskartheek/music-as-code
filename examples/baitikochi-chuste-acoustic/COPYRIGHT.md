# Copyright

"Baitikochi Chuste" is from the film *Agnyaathavaasi* (2018), music by Anirudh
Ravichander. The composition and its lyrics belong to their respective owners.

This folder holds an unofficial, synthesised arrangement made for personal
practice. It contains no audio from the original recording and no lyrics.

To sing along, put your own `lyrics.lrc` in `karaoke/`. That file and every
rendered `.wav` / `.mp4` are git-ignored and should stay out of the repository.
